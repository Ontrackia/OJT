## 573.02 Maintenance Policy Manual (MPM)

(1) An applicant for an AMO certificate shall submit to the Minister for approval a maintenance policy manual (MPM) that contains the information required by this Subpart.

## 573.10 Person Responsible for Maintenance (PRM)

(1) An AMO shall appoint a person responsible for maintenance (PRM) who meets the requirements of section 573.04.

(2) The PRM shall ensure that all maintenance releases are signed by persons authorized in accordance with the MPM.
